#PRODUCT LANDING COST AND SELLING-PRICE AUTOMATION

PROJECT OVERVIEW:
This project demonstrates how spreadsheet formulas can automate repetitive product-cost calculations and support more consistent pricing decisions.

KEY PROBLEMS:
1. Calculating the true cost of imported products manually takes time, particularly when exchange rates differs
2. Shipping charges, customs fees, other costs and particularly expense cost need to be considered across many items.

TASK:
Develop a spreadsheet workflow that calculates the landing cost of imported products in naira and supports selling-price decisions.

WHAT I DID:
1. Designed a spreadsheet structure for product information, purchase prices, exchange rates, and additional charges
2. Used formulas to convert foreign-currency costs into naira
3. Allocate relevant additional expenses AS "buying costs" 
4. Calculate the landing cost per product, and estimate selling prices based on the selected pricing rules and profit margin
5. Got a standard and suitable unit selling price for items.

RESULT:
1. The spreadsheet provides a repeatable approach to product-cost calculations 
2. Reduce the need to calculate each item manually and making cost and pricing information easier to review.

TOOLS: Microsoft Excel, spreadsheet formulas, lookup functions, and automated calculations.

KEY FEATURES:
1. Exchange-rate conversion.
2. Product landing cost calculation.
3. Allocation of shipping and other applicable charges.
4. Selling-price calculations.
5. Product-by-product cost comparison.
6. Summary of pricing figures.
